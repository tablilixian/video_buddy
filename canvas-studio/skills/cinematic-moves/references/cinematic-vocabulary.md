# 50套场景化运镜词库

按场景类型分组，每套包含：中文描述、英文描述、反向描述（静止无运动）、H3原子词映射。

---

## 一、人物出场（01-05）

### 01 缓慢推镜
- **中文**：镜头缓慢向前推进，逐渐靠近人物，突出表情变化与精神情绪，背景浅景深虚化
- **英文**：slow push-in to character, shallow depth of field, cinematic close-up
- **反向**：static camera, no movement, wide shot, flat lighting
- **H3映射**：Push In with small amplitude at slow speed toward character

### 02 快速推镜
- **中文**：摄影机快速推进到人物脸部，制造紧张压迫感，人物眼神锐利，发丝轻动，画面冲击力强
- **英文**：rapid push-in to face, tense atmosphere, sharp eyes, hair swaying, impactful frame
- **反向**：slow camera, no urgency, soft gaze, static
- **H3映射**：Push In with large amplitude at fast speed toward character's face

### 03 缓慢拉远
- **中文**：镜头从近景缓缓拉远，逐渐展示周围环境，人物孤独站立，空间感被拉开，叙事感更强
- **英文**：slow dolly out from close-up to wide, lone figure, sense of space, narrative feel
- **反向**：static close-up, no environment, tight frame
- **H3映射**：Pull Out with small amplitude at slow speed from character

### 04 环绕运镜
- **中文**：摄影机围绕人物缓慢旋转，展示服装细节与人物气场，衣摆和发丝随风摆动
- **英文**：slow orbit around character, show costume details, aura, flowing clothes and hair
- **反向**：static front view, no rotation, no detail
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot with small amplitude at slow speed around character

### 05 跟随镜头
- **中文**：摄影机跟随人物向前移动，保持人物主体稳定，环境持续变化，形成自然跟拍感
- **英文**：follow camera with character walking, stable subject, changing environment, natural tracking
- **反向**：static camera, locked tripod, no movement
- **H3映射**：Tracking Shot with character

## 二、移动跟拍 · 对话视角（06-10）

### 06 侧面跟拍
- **中文**：摄影机从人物侧面平行移动跟拍，人物缓慢行走，衣服与发丝自然摆动，形成流畅运镜感
- **英文**：side tracking shot, character walking, clothes and hair swaying, smooth motion
- **反向**：front view, no tracking, static
- **H3映射**：Truck Left/Right with character at slow speed

### 07 低角度仰拍
- **中文**：摄影机自下向上拍摄人物，强化力量感与主角气场，天空或高建筑做背景，英雄感更强
- **英文**：low angle shot, hero pose, sky or tall building background, powerful presence
- **反向**：eye level, no power, flat angle
- **H3映射**：Tilt Up from low angle toward character

### 08 高角度俯拍
- **中文**：摄影机从高处俯视人物，人物处在环境中心，突出渺小、孤独或被压迫的情绪
- **英文**：high angle overhead, character small in environment, lonely or oppressed mood
- **反向**：low angle, no scale, eye level
- **H3映射**：Tilt Down from high angle toward character

### 09 第一人称视角
- **中文**：第一人称视角拍摄，模拟角色目光，手部进入画面，环境随视线变化，沉浸感更强
- **英文**：first person POV, character eyes view, hand enters frame, environment shifts with gaze
- **反向**：third person view, no immersion, external
- **H3映射**：POV (first person perspective)

### 10 肩后视角
- **中文**：摄影机位于人物背后观察前方目标，前景肩膀轻微虚化，适合对话或对峙场景
- **英文**：over-shoulder shot, foreground shoulder slightly blurred, dialogue or confrontation
- **反向**：front view, no shoulder, direct
- **H3映射**：Over-shoulder shot, shallow depth of field

## 三、大片开场 · 空间展开（11-15）

### 11 希区柯克变焦
- **中文**：镜头前移同时背景拉伸变形，人物主体保持稳定，制造心理震荡与失衡感
- **英文**：Hitchcock zoom (dolly zoom), subject stable, background warps, psychological vertigo
- **反向**：normal zoom, flat perspective, no distortion
- **H3映射**：Push In + Zoom Out simultaneously (dolly zoom effect)

### 12 旋转下降镜头
- **中文**：摄影机环绕人物旋转并缓慢下降，展示造型与空间层次，画面更具大片感
- **英文**：spiral descent shot, orbit and lower simultaneously, show figure and space layers, epic
- **反向**：static descending, no rotation, flat
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot + Pedestal Down with small amplitude at slow speed

### 13 升降镜头
- **中文**：摄影机由低到高抬升，逐步展示完整场景，人物与环境关系被完整交代
- **英文**：crane up shot, reveal full scene, character and environment relationship
- **反向**：eye level, no scale, static
- **H3映射**：Pedestal Up with large amplitude at slow speed

### 14 无人机俯冲
- **中文**：航拍视角从高空快速俯冲到人物身边，空间瞬间展开，适合开场或史诗出场
- **英文**：drone dive shot, aerial to ground, space expands instantly, epic entrance
- **反向**：eye level entrance, no aerial, slow
- **H3映射**：Pedestal Down + Push In at fast speed (aerial dive)

### 15 穿越镜头
- **中文**：摄影机穿过门窗、树林或建筑缝隙进入场景，最后聚焦人物，沉浸感强
- **英文**：camera passes through doorway/forest/architecture gap, finally focuses on character, immersive
- **反向**：static entry, no transition, jump cut
- **H3映射**：Push In through obstacle, shallow depth of field

## 四、转场变化 · 慢镜表达（16-20）

### 16 环形推进
- **中文**：围绕人物旋转同时缓慢推进，人物保持中心，背景动态拉开，视觉张力强
- **英文**：ring push-in, orbit while slowly approaching, character centered, dynamic background
- **反向**：static front, no orbit, flat background
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot + Push In with small amplitude at slow speed

### 17 快速横移
- **中文**：镜头快速横向移动，带出运动模糊，实现人物或空间的迅速切换
- **英文**：fast horizontal pan, motion blur, rapid character or space switch
- **反向**：static frame, no blur, slow switch
- **H3映射**：Pan Left/Right with large amplitude at fast speed

### 18 甩镜转场
- **中文**：摄影机快速甩动形成方向性模糊，用运镜连接上下场景，转场利落
- **英文**：whip pan transition, directional motion blur, connect scenes, sharp transition
- **反向**：hard cut, no blur, static transition
- **H3映射**：Pan Left/Right with large amplitude at fast speed (whip pan)

### 19 慢动作升格
- **中文**：高帧率慢动作拍摄，动作被拉慢，发丝与衣摆细节更清晰，情绪更浓
- **英文**：slow motion high frame rate, slowed action, hair and cloth details clear, emotional
- **反向**：real-time speed, no detail, rushed
- **H3映射**：Static Shot + slow motion description in action

### 20 时间冻结
- **中文**：时间暂停在关键动作瞬间，摄影机环绕人物展示细节，空间粒子静止悬浮
- **英文**：time freeze at key action moment, camera orbits character, particles suspended in space
- **反向**：normal speed, no freeze, flowing motion
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot around frozen character, particles suspended

## 五、出招爆发 · 近战节奏（21-25）

### 21 拔剑特写
- **中文**：聚焦手部与剑柄，剑刃缓慢出鞘，金属反光明亮，再切人物眼神
- **英文**：close-up on hand and sword hilt, blade slowly unsheathes, metal reflection, cut to character's gaze
- **反向**：no detail, blurry hand, quick unsheathe
- **H3映射**：Push In with small amplitude at slow speed toward hand and sword

### 22 挥剑跟随
- **中文**：摄影机跟剑锋移动，剑气划过空气，人物转身攻击，打击感直接
- **英文**：camera follows blade trajectory, sword qi cuts air, character turns to attack, direct hit feel
- **反向**：static sword, no follow, soft attack
- **H3映射**：Tracking Shot following blade movement

### 23 拳击冲击
- **中文**：镜头跟随拳头冲向对方，冲击瞬间轻微震动，力量感更强
- **英文**：camera follows fist toward opponent, slight shake at impact, stronger power
- **反向**：static impact, no shake, soft hit
- **H3映射**：Push In + Shake Slightly at impact moment

### 24 闪避旋转
- **中文**：人物快速侧身闪避，摄影机同步环绕旋转，突出速度与身法变化
- **英文**：character quick dodge, camera synchronous orbit, speed and movement change highlighted
- **反向**：no dodge, static, no rotation
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot with character dodging at fast speed

### 25 跳跃追踪
- **中文**：摄影机跟随人物跃起，从地面到空中连续追踪，动作线完整流畅
- **英文**：camera follows character leap, ground to air continuous tracking, complete action line
- **反向**：static jump, no tracking, cut between
- **H3映射**：Tracking Shot + Pedestal Up following character leap

---

## 六、冲刺爆破 · 战斗高潮（26-30）

### 26 冲刺推进
- **中文**：人物高速向前冲刺，摄影机低机位贴地跟随，地面速度感明显
- **英文**：character high-speed sprint forward, camera low angle ground-hugging follow, ground speed obvious
- **反向**：static sprint, eye level, no speed
- **H3映射**：Tracking Shot at fast speed, low angle ground level

### 27 爆炸冲击
- **中文**：爆炸瞬间镜头震动，碎片飞散，人物逆光站立，适合高潮场面
- **英文**：explosion impact, camera shake, debris flying, character backlit standing, climax scene
- **反向**：no shake, calm, no debris
- **H3映射**：Shake Strongly at explosion moment, static character

### 28 子弹时间
- **中文**：动作定格在高速瞬间，摄影机环绕移动，主体清晰、空间拉满
- **英文**：bullet time, action frozen at high-speed moment, camera orbits, subject clear, space full
- **反向**：normal speed, no freeze, no orbit
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot around frozen action, slow motion

### 29 落地震动
- **中文**：人物从高处落地，镜头同步下落并轻微震动，撼尘四起，力量感强
- **英文**：character falls from height, camera synchronous drop with slight shake, dust rises, powerful
- **反向**：soft landing, no shake, no dust
- **H3映射**：Pedestal Down + Shake Slightly at landing

### 30 战斗环绕
- **中文**：摄影机 360 度环绕双方战斗，动作连续衔接，适合高潮对决
- **英文**：camera 360 orbit around both fighters, continuous action connection, climax duel
- **反向**：static fight, no orbit, flat
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot 360° around fighters at medium speed

## 七、心理放大 · 情绪表达（31-35）

### 31 眼神推进
- **中文**：缓慢推近至人物眼睛，突出瞳孔反光与情绪变化，适合心理戏
- **英文**：slow push-in to character's eyes, pupil reflection and emotion change highlighted, psychological
- **反向**：wide shot, no emotion, no eye
- **H3映射**：Push In with small amplitude at slow speed toward character's eyes

### 32 泪滴特写
- **中文**：聚焦眼角泪水滑落，背景虚化，细腻表达委屈、失落或释然
- **英文**：close-up on tear sliding from eye corner, background blur, subtle grievance/loss/release
- **反向**：no tear, dry eye, wide shot
- **H3映射**：Push In with small amplitude at slow speed toward tear

### 33 背影远寓
- **中文**：固定镜头中人物背影慢慢远去，空间逐渐空下来，离别感更强
- **英文**：character's back slowly walks away in fixed camera, space empties, sense of farewell
- **反向**：front view, no walking, close space
- **H3映射**：Static Shot, character walking away (pull-out effect via character movement)

### 34 窗边侧拍
- **中文**：从侧面拍摄人物靠窗状态，光线柔和洒落，氛围安静克制
- **英文**：side shot character by window, soft light falls, quiet restrained atmosphere
- **反向**：front view, harsh light, loud
- **H3映射**：Static Shot, side angle, soft window light

### 35 手部细节
- **中文**：聚焦手部握物、松手或轻触动作，用细节承接剧情信息
- **英文**：close-up on hand gripping/releasing/light touch, detail carries plot info
- **反向**：wide shot, no hand, no detail
- **H3映射**：Push In with small amplitude at slow speed toward hand

## 八、转场物 · 细节叙事（36-40）

### 36 物品转人物
- **中文**：从重要物件特写开始，镜头缓慢移动到人物脸部，完成情节连接
- **英文**：start from important object close-up, camera slowly moves to character's face, plot connection
- **反向**：jump from object to person, abrupt
- **H3映射**：Push In from object to character face, slow speed

### 37 镜面反射
- **中文**：通过镜子或反射面观察人物，摄像机轻移动，适合隐秘心理场景
- **英文**：observe character through mirror or reflective surface, camera light move, hidden psychology
- **反向**：direct view, no reflection, open
- **H3映射**：Static Shot through mirror, slight movement

### 38 门后窥视
- **中文**：镜头藏在门边或遮挡物后观察人物，前景虚化，紧张感自然增强
- **英文**：camera hides behind door or obstruction observing character, foreground blur, tension builds
- **反向**：direct observe, no hide, open
- **H3映射**：Static Shot from behind obstacle, shallow depth of field

### 39 雨中慢镜
- **中文**：细雨落下，人物站在雨里，镜头缓慢环绕，情绪压抑又克制
- **英文**：fine rain falls, character stands in rain, camera slowly orbits, repressed yet restrained emotion
- **反向**：sunny, dry, no rain, fast
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot around character in rain at slow speed

### 40 风吹人物
- **中文**：风吹动头发与衣角，摄影机轻缓移动，人物气质与氛围同时被放大
- **英文**：wind blows hair and clothes, camera light slow movement, character's aura and atmosphere amplified
- **反向**：no wind, still hair, static
- **H3映射**：Static Shot with wind effect, slight camera movement

## 九、仙侠奇幻 · 唯美场景（41-45）

### 41 云层穿越
- **中文**：摄影机穿过云雾下降到人物场景，仙侠或奇幻开场氛围强
- **英文**：camera passes through clouds descending to character scene, xianxia/fantasy opening strong
- **反向**：no cloud, eye level, no descent
- **H3映射**：Pedestal Down through clouds at slow speed

### 42 花瓣环绕
- **中文**：花瓣围绕人物旋转飘落，镜头缓慢移动，适合唯美与情感戏
- **英文**：petals orbit character spinning falling, camera slow move, beauty and emotion scene
- **反向**：no petal, static, harsh
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot around character with petals, slow speed

### 43 火焰环绕
- **中文**：火焰在人物周身升起，镜头环绕推进，画面冲击与能量感十足
- **英文**：flames rise around character, camera orbit push-in, impact and energy full
- **反向**：no flame, calm, static
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot + Push In around character with flames

### 44 雪花慢落
- **中文**：雪花缓慢飘落，人物立于雪中，摄影机温柔靠近，氛围清冷唯美
- **英文**：snowflakes slowly fall, character stands in snow, camera gentle approach, cold beauty atmosphere
- **反向**：no snow, warm, harsh
- **H3映射**：Push In toward character in snow at slow speed

### 45 月光移动
- **中文**：月光随镜头转移打在人物身上，层次神秘，适合夜戏与幻想场景
- **英文**：moonlight shifts with camera onto character, mysterious layers, night or fantasy scene
- **反向**：daylight, flat light, no mystery
- **H3映射**：Pan/Tilt with moonlight shift effect

## 十、都市赛博 · 史诗收束（46-50）

### 46 霓虹穿梭
- **中文**：摄影机穿过霓虹街景快速移动到人物面前，赛博与都市感突出
- **英文**：camera passes through neon street fast move to character, cyberpunk and urban feel prominent
- **反向**：daylight scene, no neon, slow approach
- **H3映射**：Push In through neon street at fast speed

### 47 水面倒影
- **中文**：从水面倒影开场，镜头上移或前移，逐步显露出真实人物
- **英文**：start from water surface reflection, camera tilt up or forward, gradually reveal real character
- **反向**：direct start, no reflection, abrupt
- **H3映射**：Tilt Up from water reflection to character

### 48 服装纹理运镜
- **中文**：从服装面料、刺绣或配饰特写开始，缓慢推到全身造型，适合角色展示
- **英文**：start from costume fabric/embellishment/accessory close-up, slowly push to full body, character display
- **反向**：full body start, no detail, abrupt
- **H3映射**：Pull Out from costume detail to full body at slow speed

### 49 史诗开场
- **中文**：超广角先展示巨大环境，再慢慢推进到人物中心，气势足
- **英文**：ultra wide first show massive environment, slowly push to character center, momentum full
- **反向**：close-up start, no scale, eye level
- **H3映射**：Pull Out to wide + Push In to character (establishing shot)

### 50 终极展示
- **中文**：摄影机 360 度环绕人物，完整展示动作、造型、表情与场景，用作结尾最合适
- **英文**：camera 360 orbit character, fully show action/look/expression/scene, best for ending
- **反向**：static end, no orbit, flat
- **H3映射**：Arc Shot 360° around character at medium speed
